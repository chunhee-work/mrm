# 10 · Car models: which items each model has, and what they are called

Read this when a case's model is known (photo naming, step 3) and again before Select Parts (step 9).
It records 冷门 (rare) items, Inner/Outer layouts and the catalogue's own name for a photo name, per model.
**After every case, add what you learned** (user 2026-10-23): new rare items, Inner/Outer, catalogue names, items the
catalogue does not have (manual row + price used). Newest entry wins.

Format per model: Merimen model text · case(s) · photo name → catalogue item (or "manual").

## Toyota
### Vios 2023 To 2026 4 Dr Sal 1.5 (NGC102) · CCQ6119
- **Rear Bumper Spoiler** (photo name) = catalogue **Bumper Rear Lower Cover**. The catalogue has no spoiler item (user 2026-10-23).
- **Blind Sport ECU LH + RH** (photo REAR RADAR DISTANCE SENSOR): no catalogue item → manual rows. Used 2,865.00 each (Almera reference, 1 row: confirm).
- Bumper Reflector Rear LH/RH: catalogue items exist (70@). The red pieces sit on the lower spoiler.
- Rear lamps: Inner (on the boot lid) LH/RH + Outer LH/RH; Inner RH = 120@81580BZ280.
- Rear Bumper Sensor (catalogue, one row, qty 2, comment).
### Corolla Altis 2014 On 1.8 E (ZRE172) · BMN7769
- Rear bumper side spoiler pieces = **Spoiler Bumper Rear LH / RH** (user's name).
- Bumper Bracket Rear LH/RH [Reinforcement] (70@ …2160 / …2190) for "Rear Bumper Bracket Side LH/RH".
- A/C Discharge Hose = catalogue "Air Conditioning Hose [Discharge]" (20@).
### Hilux 2005 To 2016 Double Cab 2.5 G (A) · WVH6628
- Ladder frame: no Suspension Crossmember item. Don't add one from the shock-absorber rule.
- Quarter Pillar LH (pickup bed pillar). Side Step LH/RH. Tread Plate Front RH = Runningboard Panel Tread Plate Front RH.
- Wheel arch pieces: Extension Wheel Arch Front/Rear LH; Moulding Wheel Arch Rear LH [Pad].
- Lower arm = Wishbone Lower Front LH/RH (110@/30@).
### Avanza 1.5 G (MPV) · SK64A
- Tailgate MPV: Tailgate Spare Wheel Carrier. Quarter Liner LH/RH.

## Honda
### Jazz 2014 On 5 Dr Hatch 1.5 (GK) · NDG4731, BPA5858
- **4 rear sensors**: Rear Bumper Sensor Inner LH/RH + Outer LH/RH → always write Inner/Outer (user 2026-10-23). Catalogue: one "Bumper Rear Sensor" row, qty = damaged count, comment lists positions.
- **Rear lamps**: Outer LH/RH (on the body) + Inner LH/RH (on the tailgate). Inner not in the catalogue → manual (465.60 each, City reference).
- Badges: **Tailgate Badge (JAZZ)**, **Tailgate Badge (i-VTEC)**; catalogue: Badge Boot Lid ['JAZZ'], Emblem Boot/Tailgate.
- Rear quarter outer = catalogue **Bodyside Rear Section LH/RH**.
- Rear Bumper Bracket Side LH/RH = catalogue **Bumper Rear Retainer/Guide LH/RH**.
- Tailgate aperture seal = catalogue "Tailgate Weatherstrip". Tailgate finisher = **Tailgate Finisher** (= Tailgate Moulding).
- Spare Wheel Foam; Boot Storagetray (manual ~155).
- No catalogue Rear Wheel House Inner → manual (64.70, weak reference).
### City 2014 On 4 Dr Sal 1.5 E · AKF8382
- Badges: **Boot Lid Badge (CITY)**; catalogue Emblem Boot/Tailgate [City] and [i-VTEC] are separate values.
- Rear lamps Inner (boot lid) LH/RH + Outer LH/RH.
- Rear Lamp Panel LH: no catalogue part → labour only.
